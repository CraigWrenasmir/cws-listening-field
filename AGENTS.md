# CWS / First Studies

This is Craig Warren Smith's piano library, developed with Maple. Preserve the approved Listening Field design and the source music.

- Read `data/STYLE.md` before composing or engraving. Straight piano, two hands, sweet melancholy, warm isolation, gentle impressionist flow and independent voices. Velvet Estuary is the primary reference.
- Every composition needs a two-word title, the next CWS opus number and a documented musical ancestor. Compose complete pieces deliberately; do not fill the catalogue with arbitrary note permutations.
- Craig's latest direction supersedes the original beginner/intermediate limit: from Op. 21, move progressively towards more experimental and advanced repertoire. Preserve the earlier music, and record/review new technical demands individually. See `data/EXPERIMENTAL_ARC.md`.
- First Studies (Op. 1–200): maximum four pages and 256 sounded pitch onsets. Second Studies (Op. 201–400): maximum six pages and 768 sounded pitch onsets. Use data/series.json. Count each chord pitch and repeated attack; exclude tied continuations.
- Retain the correct CWS / FIRST STUDIES or CWS / SECOND STUDIES header, title, opus and conventional musical markings. No subtitles, dedication line or prose playing instructions on the score. Preserve numeric composition stamps across revisions.
- Preserve existing recordings unless a musical/audio change is requested. New recordings use the warm-room settings in `data/library_style.json`.
- Explicit bar definitions are in `scripts/compose.py`, `scripts/new_pieces.py` and `scripts/dream_pieces.py`. Select opus numbers when running the music pipeline so an addition does not regenerate approved earlier pieces.
- Review engraving warnings and verify that every declared pedal span has a nonempty rendered SVG group, as well as matching MusicXML/MIDI timing. Extremely short pedal spans can be omitted by the engraver; revise the musical pedalling and recording together when that happens.
- Run the relevant music validation and render/inspect every changed PDF page. After a reflow, the PDF and numbered SVG page sets must agree exactly. The engraver removes obsolete numbered pages only after successfully writing the replacement score; `scripts/test_engraving_pages.py` checks cleanup and preservation of unrelated SVG files. `scripts/prepare_library.py` packages the gallery and downloads after those checks.
- Optional `rh_inner` rows create a separately notated right-hand inner voice; `lh_upper` rows create a separately notated left-hand tenor voice. These can be used independently or together. Use explicit `motif.voice` and `ancestry.source_voice` when selecting fragments from a polyphonic hand. Validate simultaneous whole-hand reach and pitch overlap, not just individual chord shapes. Preserve each voice in notation, playback, highlights and the sculpture. Source voice labels are `upper`, `inner`, `bass` and `tenor`; `performance.tenor_entries` and `performance.inner_entries` can bring those voices forward at specific phrases.
- Optional `hidden_voice_rests` maps `inner` or `tenor` to bars whose additional voice is silent for the whole bar while the main voice sounds. Hide only those redundant full-bar rests, preserving their MusicXML duration and voice assignment. Validate the exact nonprinting rest IDs and preserve the recording when this is an engraving-only correction. Check each rest against its actual staff and additional-voice identity; exported MusicXML voice numbers can differ between three- and four-voice layouts. `scripts/test_hidden_voice_rests.py` rejects reassignments to the bass or upper staff in the real Op. 99 fixture.
- Optional `lower_sections` gives the LH its own dynamic changes, beginning at bar 1. The ordinary `sections` then applies to the RH; without this optional field the previous shared behaviour remains. The recording retains within-hand voicing and records each event's `notated_dynamic`. Validate printed staff marks, event dynamics and actual MIDI velocities. Preserve that field during engraving-only revisions; rebuild audio after musical dynamic changes. `scripts/test_staff_dynamics.py` checks real Op. 108 and rejects incorrect printed, performed and MIDI dynamics.
- Optional `voice_phrases` gives an `inner` or `tenor` voice exact quarter-beat start/end boundaries and a small `swell` from -7 to 7. Spans must not overlap in the same voice and must begin/end on complete sounding events. Engraving uses the final tied fragment as the slur endpoint. The specified voice receives its own phrase swell instead of the shared phrase arc within those spans; rebuild the recording when changing this feature. `scripts/test_voice_phrases.py` checks actual Op. 109 slurs, invalid boundaries/overlaps and independently calculated performance velocities.
- Independent phrase slurs may begin or end on chords. The validator resolves source-event IDs to each chord’s first written pitch and to the final tied fragment, rather than requiring a bare single-note ID. The real Op. 121 additions in `scripts/test_voice_phrases.py` verify all three dyad phrases and reject a premature endpoint; the earlier Op. 109 single-note and tied-phrase cases remain covered.
- When `voice_phrases` is present, `slur_engraving.py` normalises exported slur numbers after matching their original staff, voice and number. music21 can reuse a number in overlapping voices. Reserve each replacement number for whole occupied measures because XML serialises voices in groups; preserve both endpoints and all musical data. `scripts/test_slur_numbers.py` reproduces the real four-voice Op. 114 collision, verifies all sixteen pairs and rejects an orphaned endpoint.
- Optional `meters` lists one time signature per bar, beginning with `meter`. `meter_plan.py` supplies cumulative quarter-beat boundaries for notation, audio, pedal and gallery duration. Mixed-metre metadata uses `beats_per_bar: null` and explicit bar lengths/offsets; never multiply a fixed bar length for these works. Validate actual MusicXML and MIDI time-signature changes and every independent voice’s bar length.
- Optional `clef_changes` maps a hand to explicit bar-number/clef pairs (`treble` or `bass`), including an opening override at bar 1. Keep each note in its written hand and verify the exported clef changes; clef changes must not transpose the sounding pitches.
- Optional `hand_crossings` declares a bounded interval in quarter beats with RH below LH, using `start_beat`, `end_beat`, `rest_before` and `rest_after`. Both hands must have written silence in the preparation/return intervals, and the crossing must return to normal hand order. Add `hand_labels` maps for both hands at the crossing entry and normal return bars (`m.d.` for RH, `m.s.` for LH), together with appropriate clef changes. Validate exact printed labels, transition rests, hand order and reach. An optional `max_transition_leap` permits a reviewed larger move only at the crossing entry or return after the declared rest; ordinary moves retain the usual `technique_limits.melodic_leap` ceiling. Review those larger moves individually. `scripts/test_hand_crossings.py` checks the real Op. 88 fixture and rejects undeclared crossings, occupied transition rests, missing labels and misuse of transition exceptions in an isolated copy.
- Optional `tuplet_spans` explicitly groups equal eighth-note tuplets within one bar by hand, optional voice, start/end beat, actual/normal counts and stem direction. The XML export preserves exact timing while setting the correct normal note type, one bracket and a continuous beam. Validate the exported bracket endpoints, normal type, stems and beam sequence. Export MusicXML with divisions that exactly represent every written duration. The scoped `write_musicxml` helper extends the default only when needed (110880 for Op. 102 elevenths), restores it after success or failure, and is covered by `scripts/test_musicxml_precision.py`. Set `show_number="both"` to print the full ratio. Optional `placement="below"` moves an inner-voice tuplet bracket away from a held upper tie; the default remains above. Validate the chosen placement and inspect the resulting staff spacing. For 7:6 eighths, the exporter converts music21’s equivalent dotted-eighth 7:4 spelling to undotted eighths; this guarded change preserves the sounding duration, and validation rejects residual dots.
- When a score has more than six explicit tuplet groups, `tuplet_engraving.py` reuses bracket identifiers 1–6 across bars, reserving each identifier for the whole occupied bar because voices are serialised separately. Scores with six or fewer groups keep their original numbering. Validate paired endpoints and distinct numbers within each bar. `scripts/test_tuplet_numbers.py` reproduces the Op. 120 importer failure, verifies all eight groups and score timing, preserves the earlier Op. 77 numbering, and rejects unsafe reuse.
- Optional `beam_spans` groups equal, untied single eighths, sixteenths or thirty-seconds within one bar. Specify hand, optional voice, start/end quarter beats, note_type and stem. Cover complete original beams when regrouping; the exporter rejects stranded neighbours, chords, ties and tuplets. Verify each level’s beam endpoints and stem direction, and preserve audio for engraving-only changes.
