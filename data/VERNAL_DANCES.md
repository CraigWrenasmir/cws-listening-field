# Vernal Dances — CWS Op. 271–290

Twenty individually authored Second Studies, commissioned on 18 September 2026. A deliberate change from the recent nocturnal manner: spring waltzes, skipping compound-time dances, offbeat two-steps, uneven steps and vivid dynamic contrasts. The shorter dances are balanced by the expansive Lilac Belvedere and Apricot Commons.

The music retains warm tonal centres, occasional modal and jazz colour, independent voices and precise ancestry. Tempo shaping supports a steady dance pulse; no random timing is used. Every new score and recording is for two hands at a straight piano, with the established warm room reverb.
