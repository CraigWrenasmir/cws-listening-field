# Imaginary Ensembles — CWS Op. 291–320

Thirty newly commissioned Second Studies for two hands at a straight piano. Voices retain distinct personalities across the form: proposing, answering, interrupting, and gradually finding agreement. The sound remains warm, lyrical and harmonically grounded, with more independent phrasing and chamber-jazz colour.

Op. 291–300: intimate conversations. Op. 301–310: fully notated improvisations. Op. 311–320: miniature concertos, contrasting exposed lines with fuller passages and more substantial changes of pace. Proportions and technical demands vary; six pages and 768 sounded pitch onsets remain the upper limits.

The score presentation and numeric composition stamps remain unchanged. Exact catalogue ancestry connects every work into Second Studies. Third Studies starts at Op. 401; this batch stops at 320.
