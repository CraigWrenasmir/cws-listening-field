# Returning Waters — Op. 251–270

Craig authorised twenty new Second Studies on 17 September 2026, informed by a private computational study of three of his original piano recordings. The study supplied provisional compositional prompts, not checked transcriptions or a complete account of his style. These are new compositions with separate, exact musical ancestry in the existing catalogue.

The working direction is economical pitch material, restrained sixth/seventh/ninth colours and returning harmonic anchors, with freer phrasing. Melodies can begin late, continue across changes of harmony, return at a different length, or pass between hands. Intensity can grow through texture while the harmonic palette stays recognisable. Rhythmic freedom is composed through durations, rests, independent phrases and deliberate tempo shaping.

- 251–255: recurring bass figures beneath increasingly free melodic phrases.
- 256–260: spacious chordal colours, with melody and inner movement determining the harmony.
- 261–265: larger arcs built by changing activity around returning harmonic anchors.
- 266–270: independent conversations and passages where either hand can lead.

Retain six printed pages and 768 sounded pitch onsets per work, two hands, warm room reverb, two-word titles and the numeric composition stamp. Keep every existing composition and its immutable asset URL. Both Kinship regions and all complete listening routes must include the additions. Complete and inspect each piece before the next, then publish the batch and stop at Op. 270.

Original reference recordings, their file paths, machine transcriptions and analysis remain private and are not included in the public repository. The musical and engraving checks verify structure, timing and presentation; artistic response and practical keyboard feedback remain Craig's to assess.
