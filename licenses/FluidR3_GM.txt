FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont, as rendered to per-note MP3 files by Benjamin Gleitzman's midi-js-soundfonts
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), fetched by the Hands and Free play pages for the sampled
instruments (acoustic grand piano, electric piano, harpsichord, celesta, church organ, vibraphone).
